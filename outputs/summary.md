# Findings Summary

| Area | Finding | Evidence | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stability | Plate discipline repeats reliably from season to season. | Year-to-year r: whiff .87; chase, zone swing, zone contact .81; wOBA .47; xwOBA .66 (990 pairs, 300+ PA, 2021–2026). | League-wide regulars, not prospects. |
| Forecasting | Discipline adds nothing to a next-season wOBA forecast once wOBA and xwOBA are known. | Cross-validated R²: .278 with wOBA + xwOBA; .271 adding nine discipline metrics; .048 discipline alone. | Linear models; hitters grouped across folds. |
| Prospect early signals | Early production carries modest signal; early chase rates carry none. | First 250 PA vs. PA 251–850: wOBA ρ = .36 (95% CI .02 to .63); chase rates ρ −.02 to .05. | 35 players, hand-picked cohort. |
| Readiness gates | A five-gate discipline checklist did not identify who would produce. | ρ = −.18 (95% CI −.51 to .18); mean later wOBA flat from 0 to 5 gates. | Thresholds are league medians; small sample. |
| Ben Rice | Contact quality led results, then results followed. | 2024 wOBA .269 with xwOBA .338; wOBA .358 in 2025 and .384 in 2026; 2026 All-Star. | Three seasons. |
| Anthony Volpe | Production steady below his prospect profile. | wOBA .286–.297 in every season 2023–2026; chase down from 31–32% to 27% since 2025. | 2026 began on the injured list after shoulder surgery. |
| Jasson Dominguez | Sample still short and interrupted. | .316 wOBA in 428 PA (2025); 221 PA in 2026 around an AC joint sprain and two options. | 749 career PA. |
| Team baserunning | Declined through 2024, then recovered. | BsR +7.5 (7th, 2017) → −17.1 (30th, 2024) → +3.7 (12th, 2026); 2021–2024 UBR −31.8 of −38.7 BsR. | No UBR for 2025–2026. |
| Team defense | Depends on the system. | OAA 23rd–30th in 2018–2021; DRS +36 (9th) in 2018; both strong in 2022; both mid-league 2023–2026. | OAA and DRS correlate at r = .61. |
